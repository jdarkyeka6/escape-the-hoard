No mouse buttons needed: F fires (and places), Q toggles aim, X removes a build piece. Trackpad mode raises look speed and turns on smoothing. Drop GRAPHICS to MED or LOW if the frame rate dips — it trades shadow detail and render resolution for speed.
Capture: H hides the readouts for a clean frame, P saves a PNG, O is slow motion. PG MODE keeps the impact effects at mild impact for classification. FILM MODE draws every weapon and lets [ and ] set the wave, so you can shoot the boss without playing to ten.
